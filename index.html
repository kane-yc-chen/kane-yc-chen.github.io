簡介About

我是國立臺灣大學國家發展研究所碩士生，研究興趣在公共行政與治理。碩士階段聚焦於兩個問題：國家如何透過行政法人、政府捐助財團法人等準政府組織提供公共服務，這些制度又如何變遷與引發爭議；以及能源與環境議題中，制度與公民參與如何影響治理能力。I am an M.A. student at the Graduate Institute of National Development, National Taiwan University, working on public administration and governance. My research asks two questions: how the state delivers public services through quasi-governmental bodies such as administrative corporations and government-endowed foundations, and how those institutions change and become contested; and how institutions and citizen participation shape governance capacity in energy and environmental policy.

目前於臺灣大學與臺北市立大學擔任兼任研究助理，參與國科會、醫學倫理與政府委託研究計畫。I currently work as a graduate research assistant at National Taiwan University and the University of Taipei on projects funded by the National Science and Technology Council (NSTC), a hospital–university collaboration, and government commissions.

研究領域與方法Research Areas & Methods

行政法人與準政府組織Administrative corporations & quasi-governmental organizations

制度變遷、爭議生成、組織轉型可行性，以及人事與薪資制度設計。Institutional change, how controversies arise, feasibility of organizational transformation, and personnel and pay system design.

能源安全與地緣政治Energy security & geopolitics

關鍵能資源風險、區域合作策略與城市能源韌性。Risks to key energy resources, regional cooperation strategies, and urban energy resilience.

環境治理與公民審議Environmental governance & public deliberation

環境與科技議題中的公民參與、審議機制與科技治理。Citizen participation, deliberative institutions, and technology governance in environmental issues.

東亞國會如何討論氣候損失與損害（L&D）及氣候融資。How East Asian legislatures debate climate loss and damage (L&D) and climate finance.
