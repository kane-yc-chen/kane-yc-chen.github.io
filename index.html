新興科技帶來的倫理難題，社會該如何共同討論、共同決定？我的碩士論文預計以公民審議為分析對象，探討經抽樣選出的一般民眾，如何思辨次世代定序時代產前與新生兒基因檢測的倫理與治理課題。論文之外，我研究國家如何透過行政法人與政府捐助財團法人提供公共服務、這些組織為何引發爭議；也參與地緣政治下的能源安全研究，並比較日本與韓國國會如何討論氣候「損失與損害」。 How should a society deliberate on the ethical questions raised by new technologies? My master’s thesis will analyze a citizen deliberation in which sampled members of the general public discuss prenatal and neonatal genetic testing in the next-generation sequencing era. Beyond the thesis, I study how the state delivers public services through administrative corporations and government-endowed foundations and why these bodies become contested, work on energy security under geopolitical risk, and compare how the Japanese and Korean legislatures debate climate loss and damage.

簡介About

我是國立臺灣大學國家發展研究所碩士生，關心公共制度如何治理新興科技，以及公民如何參與這些決策。我的碩士論文預計從審議民主的角度出發，分析經抽樣選出的一般民眾在公民審議中，如何思辨次世代定序時代產前與新生兒基因檢測所牽動的倫理與治理問題。I am a master’s student at the Graduate Institute of National Development, National Taiwan University, interested in how public institutions govern emerging technologies and how citizens take part in those decisions. Taking a deliberative democracy perspective, my thesis will analyze how sampled members of the general public, brought together in a citizen deliberation, reason through the ethical and governance questions raised by prenatal and neonatal genetic testing in the next-generation sequencing era.

論文之外，我也研究國家透過哪些組織形式行動。國科會計畫比較行政法人與政府捐助財團法人的制度變遷與爭議生成；職災預防及重建中心轉型評估、國家中醫研究院人事薪資制度建構兩項委託研究，則讓我從實務端理解這類組織如何被設計。此外，我參與地緣政治下能源安全與區域合作的國科會研究，並以計量文本分析比較日本與韓國國會如何討論氣候「損失與損害」。Beyond the thesis, I study the organizational forms through which the state acts. An NSTC project compares institutional change and controversy in administrative corporations and government-endowed foundations, while two commissioned studies, a feasibility assessment for the Center for Occupational Accident Prevention and Rehabilitation and the design of personnel and pay systems for the National Institute of Traditional Chinese Medicine, show how such bodies are designed in practice. I also work on an NSTC project on energy security and regional cooperation under geopolitics, and use quantitative text analysis to compare how the Japanese and Korean legislatures debate climate loss and damage.
